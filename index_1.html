⚔️ Duel Sweeper

A two-player deduction duel. Take turns. Use the numbers. Outsmart each other.

How to play
  • Two players share one board and take turns.
  • Reveal a hidden cell on your turn: safe cells earn +1 point each (chains score for every cell opened). Hit a mine and you lose 3 points — the mine stays exposed for both of you as free info.
  • Flag a cell you suspect is a mine — it's a free action (no turn used) to help you and your opponent think out loud. Right‑click on desktop, or switch to Flag Mode below.
  • The duel ends when every safe cell is revealed. Highest score wins!
  • Tap Copy Challenge Link to send the exact same board layout to a friend on another device.
